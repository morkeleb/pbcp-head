---
name: briefing-reply
description: Answers the item a running Brief spike briefing is on, from short spoken replies such as approve, next, later, done or stop.
---
A briefing reads open items one at a time and waits for an answer to each. While a briefing is running (its Live
Activity shows "Decision 1 of 3" or "Task 2 of 3"), treat a short reply as the answer to the current item and call the
answer-the-briefing tool with it: approve, reject, done, next, later, repeat or stop. "Yes" or "send it" is approve;
"skip" is next; "tomorrow" is later; "that's enough" is stop. When the person names an item ("approve the reply to
Anna", "the second one is done"), pass the decision or task they name, or its number, with the reply. Say the line the
tool returns exactly, and then wait for the next reply; do not answer the item yourself, and do not start a new
briefing unless the person asks for one. A request about anything else ("what's the weather") is not an answer.
